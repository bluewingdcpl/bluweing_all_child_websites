Products

Precast products

Hollow core slabs, wall panels, columns, beams, staircases and drainage — manufactured to fixed specification on German-engineered moulds.

Section I

Precast products for buildings.

Hollow Core Slab

Specification

Bhaarat Precast has thickness ranges for hollow core slab of 150 mm, 200 mm, 265 mm, 320 mm, 400 mm and 500 mm, with a standard width of 1200 mm.

Key advantages

  • Long Clear SpansAchieve larger column-free spaces with optimized structural systems, offering greater flexibility for modern building layouts.
  • High Load-Carrying CapacityEngineered to withstand substantial structural and imposed loads while maintaining strength, stability, and durability.
  • Up to 40% Weight ReductionOptimized structural sections significantly reduce self-weight, helping minimize foundation loads and overall structural requirements.
  • No Scaffolding, Propping or FormworkFactory-manufactured components require minimal temporary works at site, reducing construction complexity, labour, and associated costs.
  • Fast & Easy ErectionPrecast elements are designed for efficient lifting, positioning, and installation, enabling faster project completion with reduced site manpower.
  • Fire & Thermal ResistanceConcrete provides excellent fire resistance and thermal performance, contributing to enhanced safety, durability, energy efficiency, and long-term building performance.

Wall Panels

Specification

Bhaarat Precast has three types of wall panel ranges.

  1. External / Load Bearing Wall Panel thickness ranges from 125 mm, 150 mm, 160 mm and 230 mm.
  2. Shear Wall Panel thickness ranges are 300 mm & 400 mm.
  3. Internal / Non-Load Bearing Wall Panel ranges are 75 mm & 100 mm.

Key advantages

  • Superior Surface FinishPrecision-manufactured surfaces provide a smooth, uniform finish, ensuring high-quality aesthetics and consistency.
  • No Plastering RequiredThe high-quality precast finish eliminates the need for conventional plastering, saving time, material, and labour costs.
  • Putty-Free & Ready to PaintThe smooth surface minimizes finishing requirements, allowing the elements to be directly prepared for painting after necessary surface treatment.
  • Fast & Easy InstallationLightweight, factory-produced components enable quick and efficient installation, significantly reducing construction time at site.
  • Reduced On-Site MEP WorkFactory-integrated openings, sleeves, and service provisions minimize on-site MEP modifications and simplify service coordination.
  • High Fire ResistanceConcrete construction provides excellent fire resistance, enhancing building safety, durability, and long-term performance.
  • Increased Carpet AreaOptimized wall thickness and precision manufacturing help maximize usable internal space, delivering greater carpet area within the same building footprint.

Beams

Specification

Bhaarat Precast has standard types of precast & prestressed beam ranges as Rectangle Beam, ‘L’ Type Beams and Inverted ‘T’ Beams, where width size ranges from 300 mm to 1400 mm and depth / height ranges from 300 mm to 1000 mm.

Key advantages

  • Long Clear SpansEnables expansive, column-free spaces, providing greater flexibility for architectural planning and functional layouts.
  • Optimized Structural DepthEngineered sections optimize structural depth, maximizing available headroom and creating more usable internal space.
  • No Scaffolding or Propping RequiredFactory-manufactured components can be efficiently erected with minimal temporary support, reducing site complexity and labour requirements.
  • Eliminates Expensive On-Site FormworkPrecast construction minimizes or eliminates conventional on-site formwork, significantly reducing labour, material consumption, and construction time.

Columns

Specification

Bhaarat Precast has standard types of precast column ranges as Square & Rectangle Column, where width size ranges from 300 mm to 1200 mm and depth / height ranges from 300 mm to 1200 mm.

Key advantages

  • Multi-Storey Production & ErectionDesigned for efficient production and erection of structural elements for multistorey buildings, enabling faster and more streamlined construction.
  • Superior Surface FinishPrecision-manufactured elements deliver a smooth, consistent, and high-quality surface finish with excellent dimensional accuracy.
  • Enhanced Performance in Corrosive EnvironmentsHigh-quality precast concrete provides excellent durability and resistance to aggressive and highly corrosive environments, ensuring long-term structural performance.
  • Four-Sided Corbel ProvisionCan be manufactured with four-sided corbels, offering greater flexibility for structural connections and supporting beams from multiple directions.

Staircases

Specification

Bhaarat Precast has a standard type of precast staircase with Tread 300 mm, Riser 150 mm and Width ranging from 900 mm to 2400 mm.

Key advantages

  • No On-Site Formwork RequiredFactory-manufactured stair components eliminate the need for conventional formwork at site, simplifying construction and reducing overall project time.
  • Faster InstallationPrecast stair elements enable quick and efficient erection, significantly accelerating the construction process.
  • Superior Surface FinishPrecision manufacturing delivers a smooth, uniform, and high-quality finish with excellent dimensional consistency.
  • Direct Kota Stone / Tile InstallationThe accurately finished surface allows Kota stone, tiles, and other floor finishes to be installed directly, minimizing additional finishing work.
  • Reduced Site LabourOff-site manufacturing and rapid installation substantially reduce on-site manpower requirements, improving productivity and construction efficiency.
  • High Dimensional AccuracyPrecision-controlled manufacturing ensures accurate tread, riser, inclination, and floor-to-floor heights, resulting in consistent quality and easy installation.

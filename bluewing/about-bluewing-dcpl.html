About

Building complex projects. Delivering them right.

Delivering turnkey construction solutions across industrial, commercial, hospitality, institutional and infrastructure sectors.

2017

Since 2017

One team, accountable end to end.

BlueWing Design & Construction Pvt. Ltd. takes on full contractual and technical responsibility for a project — design, procurement and construction delivered under one roof, one contract, one team accountable from groundbreaking to handover.

Today the wider BlueWing Group carries three specialisms: turnkey construction through BlueWing DCPL, diaphragm wall and geotechnical works through Sygnific Infra, and precast manufacturing through Bhaarat Precast. Specialist packages stay inside the group rather than going out to unknown subcontractors.

How we work

Method, not improvisation.

Five disciplines that apply on every contract, whatever its size or sector.

Single accountability

One contract covering design, procurement, statutory approvals and execution — you hold one party responsible, not three.

Buildability first

Design review and value engineering happen before mobilisation, when they still change the cost curve.

Documented quality

We have a system in place with inspection and test plans agreed with your PMC before work starts.

Resourced programmes

Resource histograms and weekly look-aheads make the handover date a commitment, not an aspiration.

Auditable billing

Rate breakdowns and measured progress you can check line by line at certification.

Safety as a system

Induction, permits and near-miss reporting that reaches management, not just the site file.

Our journey

From one contractor to four disciplines.

Since 2017, one step at a time — each milestone below opened the capability that made the next one possible.

2017March

BlueWing Design & Construction founded

Diverted full time into core construction activities.

2017November

First 5-star hospitality project

Received the LOI for ITC Narmada, a 5-star hotel in Ahmedabad, as labour sub-contractor.

ITC Narmada

2018March

First principal contractor appointment

Received the LOI for West Coast Pharmaceutical Works Ltd. and their 6 lakh sq.ft. manufacturing facility, as principal contractor.

6L sq.ft.

2020February

Operations across Gujarat and beyond

Started operations in Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, Mehsana and Rajkot, alongside projects in Hyderabad and Goa.

2022June

Sygnific Infra Pvt. Ltd. founded

A wholly owned subsidiary diversifying into diaphragm wall operations PAN India — the only construction company in Gujarat operating in both building construction and diaphragm wall.

D-Wall PAN India

2023September

Triple ISO certification

Certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 — quality, environment and occupational health and safety.

ISO 9001 · 14001 · 45001

2026March

Bhaarat Precast Pvt. Ltd. established

A wholly owned subsidiary of BlueWing DCPL, making this the only group in India operating across all three verticals — building, diaphragm wall and precast.

2026August

Launch of AluTek – Building Envelope Systems

Registered in August 2026, AluTek marks BlueWing’s expansion into innovative building envelope and façade solutions.

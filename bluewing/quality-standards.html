Our quality management system

Six controls on every project.

Applied identically whether the work is a slab cycle or a 32 m diaphragm wall panel.

01

Inspection and test plans

ITPs issued per structural element and agreed with the client's PMC before work starts.

02

Defined hold points

Work stops until inspection is signed off — no proceeding at risk.

03

Material approval trails

Every batch traceable to an approved source and test certificate.

04

Third-party testing

Concrete, steel and critical assemblies tested independently.

05

Non-conformance closure

NCRs closed at root cause, not merely rectified on the surface.

06

As-built documentation

Full handover records and defect liability support after completion.

Where work does not meet specification it is redone. Rework is a cost we absorb, not a corner we cut.

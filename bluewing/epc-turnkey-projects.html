Core service

EPC / Turnkey projects

Engineering, procurement and construction managed as a single scope — from drawing board to occupancy, without handoffs between contractors.

One contract

Whole project.

We take on full contractual and technical responsibility for a project. Most delays start at the seams between consultant, vendor and contractor — we remove the seams.

E

Engineer

Design resolved for buildability and co-ordinated across structure, MEP and finishes before a single drawing reaches site.

P

Procure

Vendor-neutral procurement to specification, with rate transparency and material approval trails you can audit.

C

Construct

Measured progress against a signed programme, QA hold points, and a named site lead who owns the handover date.

FAQs

EPC and turnkey questions.

What clients usually ask before appointing a single contractor for the whole project.

One contract covers engineering, procurement and construction. BlueWing DCPL takes full contractual and technical responsibility for the project, from design co-ordination to handover, so you deal with one accountable party instead of separate consultants, vendors and contractors.

Design co-ordination and buildability review, statutory clearances and liaison, procurement to the approved specification, construction against a signed programme with QA hold points and weekly reporting, testing and commissioning before handover, and as-built documentation with defect liability support.

When the architect, consultants and each contractor are appointed separately, the client owns every interface between them, and most delays start at those seams. Under EPC the seams sit inside one contract, so co-ordination, clashes and scope gaps are resolved by us rather than passed back to you.

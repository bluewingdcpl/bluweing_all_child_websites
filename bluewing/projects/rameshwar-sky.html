Project Overview

A large-scale, multi-block development, delivered end to end.

Rameshwar Sky is a 5-block multi-storey development. Each block has 1 basement, a ground floor and 12 upper floors, for a total built-up area of approximately 3.50 lakh sq.ft.

BlueWing Design and Construction Pvt. Ltd. carried out the Core & Shell construction works. This covered major RCC work, foundations, the basement, retaining walls, shuttering and structural construction.

The project needed careful planning, a fast-track construction method, good resource management, strict quality control and coordination across several work fronts at once.

Key Project Challenges

Difficult ground, five work fronts and a tight pace.

Each challenge needed a solution suited to this site. The photos are from the site during construction.

01

Waterlogged foundation area

The basement foundation area was waterlogged, which made excavation and foundation preparation difficult.

SolutionThe team carried out dewatering and prepared the foundation bed, then laid rubble soling to give a stable base for the RCC foundation works that followed.
Expertise demonstratedFoundation work in difficult ground conditions, using a construction method planned for this specific site.
02

Basement & retaining wall construction

The project included approximately 18,200 sq.ft. of basement area, including retaining wall works.

ExecutionExcavation, dewatering, reinforcement, formwork and concrete placement were carefully coordinated in the restricted space of the basement.
Expertise demonstratedBasement construction, retaining walls and RCC works in challenging site conditions.
03

Multi-block construction

Building 5 blocks at the same time meant coordinating manpower, materials, machinery and several work fronts together.

SolutionThe team planned construction and allocated resources block by block. This kept work moving continuously and stopped work fronts from clashing.
04

Fast-track construction & shuttering management

A key challenge was reaching the required construction speed across many floors.

Solution
  • Aluminium shuttering was used for repetitive structural work to raise speed and productivity.
  • Plywood shuttering was used for slabs and columns that needed more flexible, custom formwork.
ResultUsing both systems balanced speed, flexibility, quality and productivity.

Project Outcome

Delivered despite difficult ground and five work fronts at once.

The ground conditions were difficult and all 5 blocks were built at the same time. Even so, BlueWing completed the Core & Shell construction works.

The team's experience with waterlogged foundations, basement construction, RCC work, retaining walls and fast-track shuttering kept productivity high without compromising quality or safety.

Completed on 21 July 2025

Key Takeaway

Rameshwar Sky shows that BlueWing can deliver large multi-block, multi-storey Core & Shell projects, including difficult waterlogged foundation and basement works. Its use of dewatering, rubble soling, aluminium shuttering and plywood shuttering reflects practical construction skill, fast-track delivery and strong project management.

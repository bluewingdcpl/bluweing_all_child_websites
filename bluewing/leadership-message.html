Welcome to BlueWing Design and Construction Pvt. Ltd. (BlueWing DCPL)

At BlueWing DCPL, we do more than build structures—we cultivate a culture rooted in trust, reliability, and shared purpose. Since our inception, we have grown into a premier, full-service civil contracting firm in Gujarat, delivering complex commercial, high-rise, institutional, and specialized geotechnical infrastructure.

Driven by second-generation leadership, our commitment to quality, and continuous engineering improvement runs deep. As the Indian economy rapidly expands, we are constantly expanding our capabilities, creating opportunities for rising talent, and pioneering modern techniques to redefine the construction landscape.

Whether you are a client looking for complete turnkey execution or a professional seeking a dynamic workplace, our goal is to deliver an exceptional experience. We aim not only to exceed our clients' expectations on every build, but also to stand out as one of the most sought-after employers in the country.

Welcome to the BlueWing family. Together, let's build the future and pave a path for shared success and sustainable growth.

Mr. Jagrut Vasoya · Founder & CMD

Our strength lies in teamwork, shared responsibility, and creating opportunities for people to grow.

At BlueWing DCPL, our journey is defined by a shared passion to take on complex challenges and redefine structural standards. Guided by a First-generation Business Entrepreneurial spirit, We have built a culture where technical excellence, innovation, and integrity form the cornerstone of every project we undertake.

We firmly believe that engineering breakthroughs begin with our people. By grounding our culture in respect, care, and collaboration, we empower every team member to contribute fresh ideas, adopt advanced construction technologies, and achieve what once seemed impossible. For us, success goes far beyond square footage—it is measured by client delight, meaningful employment opportunities, and long-term industry value.

Together with our clients, partners, and dedicated team, we are proud to embark on an endless journey of growth and success.

Mrs. Pankti Vasoya · Managing Director

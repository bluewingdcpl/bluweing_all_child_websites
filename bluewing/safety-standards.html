Assurance

Safety standards

It starts and ends with every worker going home the way they arrived.

00

Zero-harm intent

Safety is a daily practice.

Our people go above and beyond on site every day. Their safety is not left to goodwill — it is a documented system with named owners, and it applies to subcontracted labour exactly as it applies to ours.

On every BlueWing site

Seven controls, every day.

Simple actions, applied without exception, are what actually move the numbers.

01

Induction before first entry

Every worker, including subcontracted labour, inducted before stepping on site.

02

Daily toolbox talks

Held at the workface, in the language the crew actually speaks.

03

Permit to work

Required for height, excavation, confined space and hot work.

04

PPE compliance

Issued, checked and enforced — including for management and visitors.

05

Equipment inspection

Scaffolding, edge protection and lifting gear inspected on a fixed cycle.

06

Near-miss reporting

Reaches management, because near-misses are the cheapest lessons available.

07

Welfare provision

Drinking water, sanitation and first aid on every site.

08

Incident review

Root-cause review with corrective action tracked to closure.

An organisation that records only injuries is measuring failure. We treat under-reporting as the problem, not as good news.

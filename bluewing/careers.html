Why BlueWing

Engineers who own the outcome.

Our project teams are small enough that an engineer owns real scope early. If you want to be a number on a large organisation chart, we are not the right fit. If you want to run a package and be accountable for it, we probably are.

Project & planning

Site engineering

Civil, structural and MEP engineers owning packages from setting-out to handover.

QA / QC

Quality engineers running ITPs, material approvals and third-party testing.

Safety

Safety officers — NEBOSH or IOSH preferred — with authority that reaches management.

Who We Are

Building Better Envelopes for Modern Architecture

AluTek Building Envelope Systems (AluTek BES) is a specialised building envelope solutions business focused on aluminium, glass and façade systems.

Our solutions are developed to address the architectural, functional and performance requirements of each project. Whether it is a high-rise commercial development, a premium residential building, a hotel, an institutional campus or an industrial facility, we aim to deliver building envelope solutions that balance design, performance, durability and execution efficiency.

AluTek BES is a specialised business vertical associated with Bluewing Design & Construction Pvt. Ltd., A specialized building envelope company delivering engineered façade and architectural envelope solutions for modern buildings.

  • PrecisionEngineered systems and meticulous execution for architectural accuracy.
  • PerformanceBuilding envelope solutions designed around durability, functionality and building performance.
  • IntegrationCoordinated façade solutions connecting architecture, materials and execution.

Services

From Concept to Completion

AluTek aims to provide coordinated building envelope solutions through an integrated approach covering technical planning, material coordination, fabrication and project execution.

Façade Design Coordination

Coordination of architectural intent, system requirements and project specifications to develop technically feasible façade solutions.

Engineering & Technical Development

Technical development and engineering coordination of façade systems, including system detailing, design coordination, shop drawings and installation-related engineering requirements.

Fabrication & Manufacturing

Fabrication planning and processing of aluminium and glass façade components in accordance with approved drawings, specifications and project requirements.

Supply & Installation

Coordinated supply, site execution and installation of façade systems and building envelope components with a focus on workmanship, safety and quality.
